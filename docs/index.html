Why USTA is Cool

1
Every Hour Matters

Unlike traditional noon-only shading, USTA evaluates all 8,760 hours in a year. Because your building does not just exist at 12 PM.

2
5 Objectives, One Pareto

Energy, comfort, glare, survivability, actuation. We do not pick winners - we let you choose from the Pareto frontier.

3
Survive the Blackout

When the grid fails, USTA buildings stay comfortable 2.5x longer. Passive survivability is not just for doomsday preppers.

4
Latitude Matters

0-30 deg? Cooling. 45+ deg? Heating. 30-45 deg? It is complicated. We mapped the transition zones so you do not have to guess.
